# Cookie Policy
Last Updated: August 10, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how the TUWA ecosystem (including [tuwa.io](https://tuwa.io/), [demo.tuwa.io](https://demo.tuwa.io/), and the [Quasar dashboard](https://quasar.tuwa.io)) uses cookies and Local Storage to provide you with a secure, multi-chain Web3 experience.

## 1. Authentication & Session Cookies
We use strictly necessary cookies for overall authorization across the ecosystem. This includes Sign-In With X (SIWX) for multi-chain wallet authentication and the underlying Payload CMS authorization mechanism. These cookies are essential for maintaining your secure session.

## 2. Dashboard Access
If you instruct your browser to refuse these session cookies, you will not be able to log into or use the Quasar dashboard. Cookies must be enabled to authenticate and access your account securely.

## 3. Local Storage (Local-First Architecture)
The TUWA state engine (Pulsar) heavily relies on your browser's Local Storage for the complete lifecycle tracking of your transactions across networks (EVM, Solana), keeping your data on your device.

## 4. No Third-Party Tracking
We do not directly use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. However, the TUWA ecosystem integrates with essential third-party Web3 libraries (such as WalletConnect and specific wallet providers) to ensure full functionality. These third-party services may employ their own cookies or local storage mechanisms. We do not control and are not responsible for the cookies, storage, or tracking practices of these external libraries. Our internal use of storage technologies is strictly limited to functional and security requirements.
