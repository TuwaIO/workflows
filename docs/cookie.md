# Cookie Policy
Last Updated: August 10, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how the TUWA ecosystem—including [tuwa.io](https://tuwa.io/), [demo.tuwa.io](https://demo.tuwa.io/), [custom-styles.tuwa.io](https://custom-styles.tuwa.io/), and the [Quasar dashboard](https://quasar.tuwa.io)—uses cookies and Local Storage to provide you with a secure, multi-chain Web3 experience.

## 1. Authentication & Session Cookies
We use strictly necessary cookies for overall authorization across the ecosystem. This includes Sign-In With X (SIWX) for multi-chain wallet authentication and the underlying Payload CMS authorization mechanism used by the Quasar dashboard. These cookies are essential for maintaining your secure session and identity.

## 2. Platform Specifics
- **Quasar Dashboard ([quasar.tuwa.io](https://quasar.tuwa.io)):** Relies on session cookies for authentication. If you instruct your browser to refuse these cookies, you will not be able to log into or use the dashboard.
- **Custom Styles ([custom-styles.tuwa.io](https://custom-styles.tuwa.io)):** This tool is purely client-side and does not use functional or tracking cookies. It relies on local state and standard caching.

## 3. Local Storage (Local-First Architecture)
Our open-source state engine (Pulsar) heavily relies on your browser's Local Storage (or IndexedDB) for the complete lifecycle tracking of your transactions across networks (EVM, Solana). This ensures your transaction state remains on your device in a Local-First manner. We encourage developers to read the [Pulsar Documentation](https://tuwa.io/docs) for technical details on this architecture.

## 4. No Third-Party Tracking
We do not directly use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. However, the TUWA ecosystem integrates with essential third-party Web3 libraries (such as WalletConnect and specific wallet providers) to ensure full functionality. These third-party services may employ their own cookies or local storage mechanisms. We do not control and are not responsible for the storage or tracking practices of these external libraries.
