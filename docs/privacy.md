# Privacy Policy
Last Updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FOP Tkach Oleksandr collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the unified TUWA ecosystem, including the [Quasar dashboard](https://quasar.tuwa.io), the main site [tuwa.io](https://tuwa.io/), and [demo.tuwa.io](https://demo.tuwa.io/).

## 1. Core Principle: Self-Custody
Our philosophy is Purist Web3. We operate on a Local-First and Self-Custody basis. We NEVER ask for, access, or store your private keys. You maintain full sovereignty over your digital assets.

## 2. Data Collection & Notifications
To provide the ecosystem's features, we collect limited data. During initial registration, we collect your email address strictly for sending necessary notifications. We also collect public wallet addresses and transaction metadata to synchronize your history across devices. Additionally, if you choose to utilize our paid services, we may collect necessary billing and payment information to process transactions and maintain your billing history. We never share your data with third parties.

## 3. API Keys & Infrastructure
We utilize robust RPC infrastructure to process requests. Users have the option to provide their own custom RPC API keys. Any custom API keys you provide are strictly encrypted in our database. Only you can view and decrypt your key.

## 4. Data Storage and Protection
Most operations occur locally on your client device using Local Storage. Any synchronized metadata is protected using industry-standard security measures.

## 5. Your Rights
You have the right to access the data associated with your account and request its complete deletion from our infrastructure at any time.
