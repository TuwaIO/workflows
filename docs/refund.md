# Refund Policy
Last Updated: October 14, 2026

This Refund Policy explains when and how FOP Tkach Oleksandr ("we," "us," or "our") refunds payments for Quasar Cloud quota ("Quota") bought in the Quasar dashboard at [quasar.tuwa.io](https://quasar.tuwa.io/). It is part of our [Terms of Service](https://tuwa.io/terms). Quasar Cloud and its Quota are sold only to businesses (Terms of Service, Section 1). Our open-source npm packages and Quasar Community Edition are free, so there is nothing to refund for them.

## 1. How Quota Works
- **Prepaid units.** Quota is bought in advance as units. The price of a unit, any volume discount and the total are shown at checkout before you pay.
- **No expiry, no subscription.** Units never expire. Quota is a one-off purchase ("top-up"): there is no subscription and no automatic charge.
- **Paid in cryptocurrency.** You pay an invoice from a crypto wallet (the "payer wallet"). Every top-up has a receipt in Billing → History.
- **Order of spending.** Units are spent in the order they were added: the sign-up bonus first, then the oldest top-up. A new top-up therefore stays whole for as long as possible.

## 2. The 14-Day Window
You can ask for a refund of the unused part of a top-up within 14 calendar days after its payment was confirmed on-chain. After 14 days the top-up can no longer be refunded under this voluntary policy (except for our errors, see Section 6), but its units stay on your balance and never expire. This time limit does not cut off any mandatory rights described in Section 9.

## 3. What We Refund
- **The unused part of the top-up.** The unused units of a top-up are your balance minus the units of newer top-ups, but never less than zero and never more than the units of that top-up.
- **The amount.** The amount you paid × unused units / units in the top-up, at the discounted price you actually paid, in the currency of the invoice, rounded to the nearest cent.
- **RPS.** If the top-up was a pack with extra requests per second (RPS), that RPS is removed together with the refund. Extra RPS bought on its own in the top-up is refunded for the days left in its 30-day period.

Example: you have the 100-unit sign-up bonus, buy 10,000 units for $19.99 and then use 2,500 units. The bonus covers the first 100 units and the top-up the other 2,400, so 7,600 units of the top-up are unused and the refund is $19.99 × 7,600 / 10,000 = $15.19.

## 4. What We Do Not Refund
- Units that have already been used, under this voluntary refund policy.
- The sign-up bonus and promotional units.
- Top-ups paid more than 14 days ago, under this voluntary policy, except for our errors (Section 6). Their units stay on your balance.
- The network fee (gas) that the payer wallet paid to send the payment.

These exclusions do not override mandatory rights under Section 9.

## 5. How Refunds Are Sent
- **Same asset, same network, same wallet.** Under this policy, we send the refund in the asset and on the network of the payment, to the payer wallet only. We cannot send it to another address. If applicable law requires another method, we will comply with that law.
- **Exchange rate.** The refund amount is set in the currency of the invoice and converted into the asset at the exchange rate on the day of the refund. For a volatile asset, the number of tokens you receive may therefore differ from the number you paid.
- **Network fee.** We pay the network fee (gas) of the refund.
- **Confirmation.** We confirm every refund by email to the address of your account, with the refunded amount, the units removed and the refund transaction.
- **Units.** The refunded units are removed from your balance at the moment the refund is sent. If you used more units after your request, we refund the part that is still unused at that moment, under this voluntary policy. Refunds under mandatory law are governed by Section 9.

## 6. Our Errors
If you paid twice, your Quota was not added after a confirmed payment, or a payment was applied to the wrong invoice, we fix it or refund the payment in full. You can tell us about such an error within 90 days of the payment; the 14-day window does not apply. Refunds for our errors follow Section 5. These deadlines do not limit non-waivable legal remedies.

## 7. How to Request a Refund
Write to [admin@tuwa.io](mailto:admin@tuwa.io) from the email address of your account, with the organization, the receipt or transaction of the top-up and the reason. The owner or an admin of the organization can request a refund.

We answer voluntary-policy requests within 5 business days and send an approved refund within 10 business days of the request. We tell you the outcome by email: refunded (with the transaction) or declined (with the reason). Refunds under mandatory law follow the time limits of that law (Section 9).

## 8. Limits
- One voluntary-policy refund per top-up.
- We may decline voluntary-policy refunds to an organization that repeatedly buys and refunds Quota (more than 2 refunds within 90 days).
- We do not send refunds to a wallet flagged by sanctions screening, or where applicable law does not allow the payment. Where a refund cannot lawfully be sent to the payer wallet, contact us to resolve any refund owed by a legally permissible means.

These limits do not exclude non-waivable rights (Section 9).

## 9. Mandatory Rights
Quasar Cloud is sold only to businesses. If the law that applies to your purchase nevertheless gives you rights that cannot be excluded by contract, such as a statutory right to withdraw from a distance contract or remedies when a digital service is not supplied or does not conform to the contract, this policy does not limit them, and we refund what that law requires within the time it sets. If a purchase was made by a consumer, we may also cancel it and refund the payment under Section 5 of our Terms of Service.

## 10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy. The date of the current version is at the top. A refund request is handled under the version that was in force when the top-up was paid, subject to mandatory law.
