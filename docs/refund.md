# Refund Policy
Last Updated: October 23, 2026

This Refund Policy explains when and how FOP Tkach Oleksandr ("we," "us," or "our") refunds payments for Quasar Cloud quota ("Quota") bought in the Quasar dashboard at [quasar.tuwa.io](https://quasar.tuwa.io/). It is part of our [Terms of Service](https://tuwa.io/terms). Quasar Cloud and its Quota are sold only to businesses (Terms of Service, Section 1). Our open-source npm packages and Quasar Community Edition are free, so there is nothing to refund for them.

## 1. How Quota Works
- **Prepaid units.** Quota is bought in advance as units. The price, any volume discount and the total are shown at checkout before you pay.
- **No expiry, no subscription.** Units never expire. Quota is a one-off purchase ("top-up"): there is no subscription and no automatic charge.
- **How you pay.** You pay by card through our payment provider WayForPay, or by bank transfer against our invoice. The checkout shows the amount you are charged and its currency before you pay. Every top-up has an invoice and a receipt in Billing → History.
- **Order of spending.** Units are spent in the order they were added: the sign-up bonus first, then the oldest top-up. A new top-up therefore stays whole for as long as possible.

## 2. The 14-Day Window
You can ask for a refund of the unused part of a top-up within 14 calendar days after its payment was confirmed. After 14 days the top-up can no longer be refunded under this voluntary policy (except for our errors, see Section 6), but its units stay on your balance and never expire. This time limit does not cut off any mandatory rights described in Section 9.

## 3. What We Refund
- **The unused part of the top-up.** The unused units of a top-up are your balance minus the units of newer top-ups, but never less than zero and never more than the units of that top-up.
- **The amount.** The amount you were charged × unused units / units in the top-up, at the discounted price you actually paid and in the currency you were charged in, rounded down to the smallest unit of that currency.
- **RPS of a pack.** If the top-up was a pack whose price includes extra requests per second (RPS), that RPS ends when the refund is sent. It is not refunded separately: the amount above covers the whole pack.
- **RPS bought on its own.** If the top-up bought extra RPS on its own, for a 30-day period, the part of the price paid for that RPS is refunded separately, for the time left in its period: the price of the RPS × the share of its 30 days still left when the refund is approved, rounded down to the smallest unit of the currency. The units formula above applies only to the rest of the price. The extra RPS ends when the refund is sent.

Example: you have the 500-unit sign-up bonus, buy 10,000 units for ₴830.00 and then use 2,500 units. The bonus covers the first 500 units and the top-up the other 2,000, so 8,000 units of the top-up are unused and the refund is ₴830.00 × 8,000 / 10,000 = ₴664.00.

## 4. What We Do Not Refund
- Units that have already been used, under this voluntary refund policy.
- The sign-up bonus and promotional units.
- Top-ups paid more than 14 days ago, under this voluntary policy, except for our errors (Section 6). Their units stay on your balance.
- Fees and currency conversion that your bank or card issuer applies to the payment or the refund.

These exclusions do not override mandatory rights under Section 9.

## 5. How Refunds Are Sent
- **Card payments.** A card payment is refunded through WayForPay to the same card, in the currency of the original charge, so the amount does not depend on any exchange rate on the day of the refund. Your card issuer may convert it into the currency of your card at its own rate and may charge its own fees; we cannot promise the amount you see after that. We send an approved card refund within 10 business days of your request; your card issuer may take several more business days to show it.
- **Bank transfers.** A payment by bank transfer is refunded only in the way, currency and to the account that our bank allows for that payment under the currency rules that apply to it. Before we send it, we tell you the amount we will send, the currency, the account and the bank fees we know of that may reduce what you receive. If our bank does not allow the refund, we tell you and look with you for a lawful way; until then the units stay on your balance.
- **Our fees.** We pay the fees that our payment provider and our bank charge us for sending the refund.
- **Credit note and refund confirmation.** When we approve a refund, we issue a credit note that corrects the original invoice by the refunded amount. When the money is sent, we email you a refund confirmation with the invoice number, the amount, the date and the identifier of the refund operation. Both appear in Billing → History.
- **Units.** The refunded units are removed from your balance when the refund is approved, so they cannot be spent while the refund is on its way. If you used more units after your request, we refund the part that is still unused at that moment, under this voluntary policy. Refunds under mandatory law are governed by Section 9.
- **Other payment methods.** Before we accept a payment method not listed in Section 1, we will update this policy to say how it is refunded.

## 6. Our Errors
If you paid twice, your Quota was not added after a confirmed payment, or a payment was applied to the wrong invoice, we fix it or refund the payment in full. When we refund such a payment in full, the units it added are removed from your balance as far as they are still unused; we do not charge you for any of them you already used. You can tell us about such an error within 90 days of the payment; the 14-day window does not apply. Refunds for our errors follow Section 5. These deadlines do not limit non-waivable legal remedies.

## 7. How to Request a Refund
- **In the dashboard:** Billing → History → "Request refund" next to the top-up. The owner or an admin of the organization can request it and gives a reason.
- **By email:** write to [admin@tuwa.io](mailto:admin@tuwa.io) from the email address of your account, with the organization, the invoice number and the reason.

We answer voluntary-policy requests within 5 business days. We email you when we receive your request, when we decline it (with the reason) and when we send the refund (with the refund confirmation). The status is also shown in Billing → History: Requested, then Refunded or Declined. Refunds under mandatory law follow the time limits of that law (Section 9).

**Business days** in this policy are Monday to Friday, counted in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) from the moment we receive your request; public holidays are not excluded.

## 8. Limits
- One voluntary-policy refund per top-up.
- We may decline voluntary-policy refunds to an organization that repeatedly buys and refunds Quota (more than 2 refunds within 90 days).
- We do not send a refund where applicable law, including sanctions, does not allow the payment. Where a refund cannot lawfully be sent the way you paid, contact us to resolve any refund owed by a legally permissible means.
- **Disputes and chargebacks.** We ask you to contact us before you dispute a payment with your card issuer or bank: a refund under this policy is usually faster. This is a request, not a condition. While a dispute about a top-up is open, we do not send a voluntary refund for it, so that the payment is never returned twice; we handle your refund request after the dispute ends. If a dispute returns the payment to you, the units of that top-up are removed from your balance as far as they are unused.

These limits do not exclude non-waivable rights (Section 9).

## 9. Mandatory Rights
Quasar Cloud is sold only to businesses. If the law that applies to your purchase nevertheless gives you rights that cannot be excluded by contract, such as a statutory right to withdraw from a distance contract or remedies when a digital service is not supplied or does not conform to the contract, this policy does not limit them, and we refund what that law requires within the time it sets. To use such a right, write to [admin@tuwa.io](mailto:admin@tuwa.io); you do not need the refund request in the dashboard. If a purchase was made by a consumer, we may also cancel it and refund the payment in full, the way Section 5 describes.

## 10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy. The date of the current version is at the top. A refund request is handled under the version that was in force when the top-up was paid, subject to mandatory law.
